SurveyAutoAI QA Harness Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Version 0.1.0

Core Commitment: SurveyAutoAI operates with a strict zero-data-collection architecture. We do not operate backend tracking servers, we do not log your survey responses, and we do not sell or monetize personal data. All processing is performed locally in your browser.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

SurveyAutoAI does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on external developer servers:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • No Survey Submissions or Answers: Answers planned and filled are strictly transient in local browser memory and your local storage.
  • No Browsing History: The extension only inspects the active browser tab when you explicitly trigger "Scan", "Plan", or "Speedrun".
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: No third-party tracking scripts (such as Google Analytics or Mixpanel) are bundled or executed.

2. Data Handled Locally in Your Browser

The following data items are stored entirely inside your local browser profile via chrome.storage:

  • Google Gemini API Key: Stored locally in chrome.storage.local. When passphrase protection is enabled, the key is encrypted at rest using PBKDF2 (310,000 rounds) and AES-GCM 256-bit encryption. The passphrase is never stored on disk.
  • Form Ownership Attestation Records: A local registry of origins you have registered as owning or administering for testing purposes.
  • Custom Personas: Personas and test demographic profiles you create in the Persona Builder are stored locally or synced across your signed-in Chrome profile via chrome.storage.sync.
  • Session Run History: Transient records of question/answer pairs generated during active sessions, which you can choose to export as JSON or CSV directly to your local file system.

3. Third-Party AI Gateway (Google Gemini API)

To generate test answers and reasoning according to your chosen persona, SurveyAutoAI communicates directly from your local browser runtime to Google Generative AI endpoints (https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/*).

  • Calls are authenticated exclusively with your own Google Gemini API key.
  • Only the sanitized question text, options, and previous session answers needed for anti-contradiction coherence are transmitted in the prompt payload.
  • Your interactions with the Gemini API are governed by Google's Generative AI Terms of Service.

4. Browser Permissions Justification

In adherence to the Chrome Web Store Least Privilege Principle, SurveyAutoAI requests only the minimum necessary permissions:

Permission Technical Purpose
activeTab Enables the extension to inspect and interact with the survey form in the currently focused tab upon user invocation.
storage Stores encrypted API keys, form attestation records, and custom personas in your local browser profile.
sidePanel Provides the Chrome Side Panel user interface for controlling survey scans, inspecting answers, and monitoring runs.
scripting Injects the content automation script on-demand into attested survey tabs without requiring broad <all_urls> access.
downloads Allows downloading local audit receipts (JSON or CSV export) when you click "Export Run".

5. Ethical Use & Form Ownership Gate

SurveyAutoAI is engineered strictly as a QA testing and validation harness for survey authors, developers, and organizations who administer web forms. The extension enforces a mandatory two-step ownership attestation gate before reading or filling forms, preventing unauthorized automated interaction on third-party surveys.

6. Contact & Support

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this privacy policy or the security design of SurveyAutoAI, please submit an issue on our project repository.
